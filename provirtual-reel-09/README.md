# ProVirtual PH — Reel #9: More Than Just a VA

`ProVirtual_PH_Reel_09_More_Than_Just_A_VA.mp4` — 30 s, 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, H.264 + AAC stereo.

Built entirely from original motion graphics (HTML/CSS 3D scenes rendered frame-by-frame with headless Chromium),
a Kokoro neural-TTS voiceover, and a synthesized original soundtrack + sound effects (mixed with numpy, normalized to -14 LUFS).
`source/` holds the scene code, renderer and audio scripts (Kokoro model files are downloaded separately).
All interfaces are illustrative/sample content — no real client data, stats or testimonials.
